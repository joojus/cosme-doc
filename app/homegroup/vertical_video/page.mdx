# Vertical Video / Shop the Look

The **Vertical Video / Shop the Look** section presents shoppable portrait‑format videos in a premium, editorial carousel. It is designed for beauty and lifestyle brands that want to combine immersive video content with product discovery.

The section features a dominant‑centre carousel with infinite looping, where the active video is visually larger and side videos are partially visible. Each video can be linked to a product, displaying a compact overlay with product image, title, price, and a Quick View button. Category navigation (collections) can optionally appear above the carousel.

![Vertical Video in action](/img/vertical-video-settings.png)

---

## Overview

The Vertical Video section is a full‑width, editorial‑style carousel that typically sits between the Hero and other content. It includes:

- **Vertical video cards** – portrait‑format videos (9:16 or 4:5) with autoplay on visibility.
- **Dominant‑centre carousel** – the active card is scaled up, side cards are smaller and peek partially to indicate more content.
- **Infinite loop** – the carousel seamlessly wraps around; arrows never become disabled.
- **Category navigation** – optional horizontal scrollable collection links with custom images and fit control (cover/contain).
- **Product overlay** – on each video, a compact overlay shows the product image, title, price, and a Quick View button (`+`).
- **Video controls** – play/pause and mute/unmute buttons (appear on hover/focus).
- **Autoplay** – videos autoplay when visible (viewport‑based or all videos), with support for reduced motion.
- **Carousel autoplay** – optional auto‑scroll of the carousel with adjustable delay.
- **Three colour schemes** – Light, Dark, Light+Accent, fully integrated with the global colour system.
- **Fully responsive** – adapts to desktop, tablet, and mobile with native touch scrolling.

All settings are available directly in the Shopify Theme Editor – no code required.

## Blocks

The section uses two block types: **Collection Link** (for category navigation) and **Video** (for each video card).

| Setting | Type | Default | Description |

| Setting | Type | Default | Description |

## Visual Layout

### Desktop (≥1024px)

- The heading and optional eyebrow sit at the top, aligned left or center.
- Category navigation (if enabled) appears as a horizontally scrollable row of small square cards.
- The video carousel shows the number of cards set in **Desktop cards**.
- The **active (centre) card** is scaled to 100% (full size); side cards are scaled down (~88%) and partially visible (about 50% of their width).
- The carousel loops infinitely; arrow buttons always work (never disabled).
- Video controls (play/pause, mute/unmute) appear on hover or when the card is active.

### Tablet (768–1023px)

- The carousel shows 3 cards (fixed).
- Category navigation remains horizontally scrollable.

### Mobile (768px)

- The carousel shows 1 or 1.5 cards (as set in **Mobile cards**).
- The next card is partially visible to indicate scrolling.
- Touch scrolling works natively; video controls are always visible (no hover).
- Category navigation scrolls horizontally with touch.

## Carousel & Video Behaviour

### Dominant‑Centre Carousel

- The active card is centred and scaled to 1; side cards are scaled to 0.88.
- The transition between cards is smooth and respects the global animation setting.
- The carousel is **infinite**: when you reach the last card and click “Next”, it seamlessly jumps to the first card.

### Auto‑Scroll Carousel

- When **Auto‑scroll carousel** is enabled, the carousel advances by one “card group” (the number of visible cards) at the set delay.
- Auto‑scroll pauses when the user hovers over the carousel (desktop) or touches it (mobile) and resumes after interaction stops.
- Clicking an arrow resets the auto‑scroll timer.

### Video Autoplay

- Videos are **lazy‑loaded** – they start loading only when they approach the viewport.
- **Autoplay** is controlled by the **Autoplay** setting and respects `prefers-reduced-motion` and the global `enable_animations` setting.
- **Autoplay behaviour:**
  - **Videos in view:** only the active (centre) video plays; all other videos are paused. When the carousel moves, the previous video pauses and the new active video starts playing.
  - **All videos:** any video that is sufficiently visible (intersection ratio ≥ 35% or 55% depending on behaviour) plays; videos that are not visible are paused.
- Videos are **muted by default**; users can unmute them individually via the mute/unmute button.
- When the browser tab becomes hidden, all videos pause; they resume when the tab becomes visible (if autoplay is allowed).

### Video Controls

- **Play/Pause** button – toggles playback; visible on hover/focus (or always on touch devices).
- **Mute/Unmute** button – toggles audio; also visible on hover/focus.

## Accessibility

The Vertical Video section is built with accessibility as a priority:

- **Semantic HTML** – uses `<section>`, `<h2>`, `<article>`, `<nav>`, `<ul>`, `<li>` appropriately.
- **ARIA attributes** – the section has `aria-label`; the carousel uses `aria-hidden` and `inert` on cloned slides; buttons have `aria-label` and `aria-pressed` states.
- **Keyboard navigation** – all interactive elements (arrows, video controls, product links, category links) are focusable and have visible `:focus-visible` rings.
- **Focus order** – category links → carousel arrows → video controls → product links.
- **Reduced motion** – all animations respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; autoplay (both video and carousel) is disabled when reduced motion is active.
- **Video accessibility** – each video has `aria-label` and `poster` for visual reference; controls are clearly labelled.
- **Touch targets** – all buttons are at least 44px (2.75rem) for easy interaction on mobile.

## Performance

- Videos use `preload="none"` by default; they start loading only when they approach the viewport (via `IntersectionObserver` with a 200px root margin).
- Only videos that are sufficiently visible (or active in “Videos in view” mode) actually play; others remain paused.
- The carousel uses native scrolling with `scroll-snap-type: x proximity` for smooth touch interaction.
- No third‑party libraries are used – the carousel, autoplay, and video controls are all vanilla JavaScript.
- Events are cleaned up when the section is removed (`shopify:section:unload`).

## Customization Tips

- **Adjust card count** – change **Desktop cards** and **Mobile cards** to control how many videos are visible at once.
- **Change aspect ratio** – use `4:5` for slightly wider portrait videos.
- **Disable autoplay** – if you prefer users to click play manually, turn off **Autoplay**.
- **Use “Videos in view”** – for a focused experience where only the centre video plays; this reduces distraction.
- **Add custom category images** – upload your own images in each Collection Link block and choose `Cover` or `Contain` to control how they fill the square.
- **Hide arrows** – turn off **Show arrows** for a cleaner, minimal look.
- **Enable carousel auto‑scroll** – to create a continuously moving editorial showcase.
- **Override product overlay per video** – use the block‑level **Show product** toggle to hide the overlay on selected videos.

## Troubleshooting

### Videos do not autoplay

- Ensure **Autoplay** is enabled in the section settings.
- Check that the video file is uploaded and correctly selected.
- If `prefers-reduced-motion` is active in the user’s system, autoplay will be disabled (this is intentional).
- Verify that the global `enable_animations` setting is `true`.

### Only the centre video plays, but I want all visible videos to play

- Change **Autoplay behavior** from `Videos in view` to `All videos`.

### The carousel does not loop infinitely

- The carousel automatically loops when there are 2 or more videos. If you have only 1 video, looping does not apply.
- If arrows are disabled (`Show arrows` off), the carousel still loops via touch scrolling.

### Product overlay does not appear

- Ensure **Show product** is enabled at both the section level and the block level.
- Verify that a product is selected in the Video block.
- If the product is not in stock or is unpublished, the overlay may not render.

### Category images are cropped incorrectly

- In the Collection Link block, adjust the **Image fit** setting to `Contain` to see the entire image, or `Cover` to fill the square with cropping.
- You can also upload a different image that better fits the square format.

### Videos pause unexpectedly on mobile

- Mobile browsers often throttle background media; this is normal. The section attempts to resume playback when the video becomes visible again.
